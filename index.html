How do I restore my subscription?

Open the account icon on the home screen and tap Restore Purchases, or sign in with the same Apple or Google account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Swipe Me, then tap Cancel Subscription. You can also tap Manage Subscription in the app's account screen.

How do I delete my account?

Open the account icon on the home screen and tap Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account. It doesn't cancel an Apple subscription, so cancel that first if you no longer want it.

Where did my deleted photos go?

Photos you delete go to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app, where you can recover them for up to 30 days.
