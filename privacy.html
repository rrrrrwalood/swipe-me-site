Privacy Policy

Swipe Me · Last updated October 14, 2026

Swipe Me helps you clean up your photo library by swiping through photos and videos. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

Your photos stay on your phone

Swipe Me needs access to your photo library to show you your photos and videos and to delete the ones you choose. Your photos, videos and their details (such as file size and date) are processed only on your device. We never upload, copy or store your photos on our servers.

Your swipe statistics (photos kept, deleted and storage freed) are stored only on your device.

Account information

You can use Swipe Me's free features without an account. If you subscribe to Pro+, you sign in with Apple or Google so your subscription is linked to your account and can be restored on other devices. When you sign in we receive:

  • Your email address (with Sign in with Apple this can be a private relay address that hides your real email)
  • Your name, if you choose to share it
  • A user ID created for your account

We use this only to run your account and subscription. Account data is stored with our provider Supabase.

Subscriptions and payments

Payments are processed by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see or store your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active; it receives your account's user ID and your purchase history from Apple.

What we don't do

  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share your personal information for advertising.

Service providers

  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and in-app purchases
  • Google: Sign in with Google
  • Supabase: account storage
  • RevenueCat: subscription status

The subscription screen also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your device's IP address when it loads.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: open the account icon on the home screen and tap Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account data from our servers. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription; cancel it in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

If you sign out or never create an account, we hold no personal information about you.

Children

Swipe Me is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
